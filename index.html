Q: I found a bug. What should I do?
A: Email us with a description of the issue, your device model and Android version, and a screenshot or screen recording if possible.

Q: The game is crashing or running slowly.
A: Make sure you are on the latest version from Google Play, restart your device, and keep some free storage space. If the problem continues, email us your device model and Android version.

Q: I lost my progress.
A: Game progress is stored on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it. If you think this happened by mistake, contact us and we'll try to help.

Q: Why does the game show ads?
A: The game is free, and ads help us keep it free and keep making new content.

Q: How do I request a feature?
A: We'd love to hear your ideas. Email us anytime.
