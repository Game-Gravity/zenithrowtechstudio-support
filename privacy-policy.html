2. Information we collect

Our Apps are free to play and do not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details. However, our App and the third-party services it uses may automatically collect the following information:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android) and other device identifiers

  • Crash and diagnostic data: error logs and performance information

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
